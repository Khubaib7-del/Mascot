## Policy
1. No third-party model, texture, HDRI or audio is committed without a row above (source, licence, attribution, redistribution terms).
2. Research-only downloads live outside the repository and are never imported by `src/`.
3. Every `MascotDefinition` carries `license`. Cards show *Prototype* for non-production assets.
4. Competitor mascots (Meta Muse/Jolly, OpenAI Dots, xAI Grok Bot, …) informed analysis only. Brand-adjacent props are generic symbols, not marks.
